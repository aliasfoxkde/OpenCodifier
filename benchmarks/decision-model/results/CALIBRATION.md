## Findings

1. **The LLM tiers are overconfident; calibration sharpens nothing.**
   All four decoder arms fit `T > 1` (flatten): Qwen3.5-4B-Q3_K_S most
   (1.672), Qwen3.5-2B least (0.843). ECE after the fit drops 27–44%
   relative (frontier MiMo-9B: 0.048 → 0.026). Confidence read off raw
   logits is systematically too high — the exact failure D15 exists for.
2. **The sub-1GB engine rung is *under*confident** (lexical
   builtin, `T = 0.602`): its scores hedge relative to their actual hit
   rate. The correction is small (0.115 → 0.108) and in-sample; treat
   the lexical artifact as optional.
3. **The embedding rung has no calibrated confidence at all.** The gte
   zero-shot fit is degenerate: NLL falls monotonically to its T→∞
   limit, so the best calibrated predictor is the constant 0.5 — no
   finite temperature helps. **No artifact was emitted.** The honest
   reading is that embedding similarity scores are ordering evidence
   only; until the embedding rung gets a real confidence channel (e.g.
   a fitted density ratio, per D2's planned OOD work), its scores must
   gate by rank/margin, never by raw probability.
4. **Per-suite-class temperatures differ sharply** (printed by the
   fitter; e.g. Qwen3.5-4B-Q3_K_S: lexical_semantic 0.52 vs
   relational_compositional 13.2). One global T is a compromise. The IR
   has no task-class tag yet, so the engine cannot address per-class
   keys today; when it does, per-class artifacts are the follow-up.

## Method and its limits

Each run JSON records the winner probability and correctness per item,
not the full candidate distribution. The fit is therefore temperature
scaling on the winner-vs-rest margin, `q = p^(1/T) / (p^(1/T) +
(1-p)^(1/T))`, minimizing Bernoulli NLL by golden-section search
(T ∈ [0.05, 200]; NLL is convex in log T). The engine applies T to the
full distribution in log-prob space (`q_i = p_i^(1/T) / Σ_j p_j^(1/T)`,
`TemperatureCalibration::rescale`); the two coincide for the top
probability when the rest-mass moves coherently, which is the reading a
policy gate uses. Refitting from full per-item distributions (top-k
logged by the runner) is the clean fix and is noted as future work.

Fits are **in-sample**: fitted and evaluated on the same 120 items.
ECE-after values are consistency checks, not generalization claims —
the same n=120/single-seed limitation recorded in `REPORT.md` applies.
