The Pyraminx

A tetrahedron puzzle with four faces, six edges, four tips, and four centers. Scramble it, turn the faces, and ask for the layer-by-layer plan. The solver verifies every move by simulation; genuine ties go to the engine.

What decides what

  • The method is a rule. Layer-by-layer: solve the tips, solve the first layer corners, solve the edges, then parity and orientation. Every move is verified by simulation before it plays.
  • Genuine ties are the setup moves. When two setups reach the next phase in the same number of moves, the engine picks one. A refusal falls back to roster order without derailing the solve.
  • A refusal cannot derail the solve. If the engine declines or is unavailable, the solver takes the first equal option and the rung chip says so.

Honest edges

  • This is a beginner solver, not a speed solver: expect ~60 moves. The point is a readable plan, not a record.
  • Turns are instant with a face flash, not tweened — the state you see is always the exact facelet state.
  • The scoreboard is session-only; nothing leaves this tab.
